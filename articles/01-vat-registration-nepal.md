# VAT Registration in Nepal: Who Must Register and How to Do It

> **Quick answer:** A business in Nepal must register for VAT once its annual taxable turnover crosses the threshold set by the VAT Act. Some sectors must register regardless of turnover. Registration is done online through the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) portal and then confirmed at your local tax office. After registration you must issue VAT invoices and file a VAT return every month.

Value Added Tax (VAT) is one of the first compliance questions every growing business in Nepal faces. Registering too late can bring penalties and back-dated tax. Registering without understanding the obligations can create monthly filing problems. This guide covers who must register, the process, and what changes once you are registered.

## Who must register for VAT?

You generally need VAT registration if any of these apply:

1. **Your turnover crosses the threshold.** Businesses supplying goods must register once annual turnover exceeds NPR [VERIFY: goods threshold, currently understood as NPR 50 lakh — VAT Act/latest Finance Act]. Businesses supplying services, or a mix of goods and services, must register above NPR [VERIFY: services/mixed threshold, currently understood as NPR 20 lakh — latest Finance Act].
2. **You operate in a sector where registration is mandatory** whatever your turnover. [VERIFY: current list of mandatory sectors, IRD notice / VAT Rules]
3. **You want to register voluntarily**, for example because your clients are VAT-registered companies that prefer VAT invoices so they can claim input credit.

**Example:** A Kathmandu IT consultancy expects to bill NPR 25 lakh this year. Because it supplies services, it is likely to cross the services threshold and should register *before* it crosses it, not after its year-end. [VERIFY: threshold applied in example]

## Documents you will typically need

- Company or firm registration certificate (OCR / Department of Commerce / local ward)
- Memorandum and Articles of Association (for companies)
- PAN certificate, if already obtained
- Citizenship copies and photos of the proprietor, partners or directors
- Proof of business address (rental agreement or ownership document)
- Bank account details

## Step-by-step VAT registration process

1. **Apply online on the IRD portal** (ird.gov.np) and fill in the PAN/VAT registration form with business details.
2. **Note your submission number** generated by the system.
3. **Visit your Inland Revenue Office or Taxpayer Service Office** with the original documents and the submission number.
4. **Verification:** the tax officer may inspect your business premises.
5. **Receive your VAT certificate.** Display it at your place of business.

Timeline: usually a few working days once documents are complete. [VERIFY]

## Common mistakes we see

1. **Registering only after crossing the threshold by a wide margin.** The IRD can assess VAT on past sales.
2. **Not filing "nil" returns.** A return is due every month even if you made no sales.
3. **Claiming input credit on ineligible purchases**, such as certain entertainment or personal expenses.
4. **Issuing invoices without the buyer's PAN** on sales above the required limit. [VERIFY: limit]
5. **Mixing personal and business purchases** on the same bills.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is VAT registration free in Nepal?**
There is no government fee for registration itself. [VERIFY]

**Can I cancel VAT registration later?**
Deregistration is possible in specific circumstances, such as closing the business or staying below the threshold for a set period. It requires an application to the IRD. [VERIFY: conditions]

**What is the penalty for late VAT returns?**
Late filing attracts a fee and interest on unpaid tax. [VERIFY: current rates under VAT Act]

**Do I need VAT if I only sell to foreign clients?**
Exports of goods and certain services may be zero-rated, but registration and filing obligations can still apply. Get advice for your specific case.

**Is PAN the same as VAT registration?**
No. Every business needs a PAN. VAT registration is an additional registration for businesses that meet the conditions above.

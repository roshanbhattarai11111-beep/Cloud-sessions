# TDS in Nepal: Rates, Deadlines and Common Mistakes

> **Quick answer:** TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) means withholding a percentage of certain payments, such as rent, service fees, salaries, interest and dividends, and depositing it with the IRD. The deposit and e-TDS filing are due within [VERIFY: 25 days] after the end of the month in which the payment was made. Not deducting TDS makes *you* liable for the tax, plus interest and fees.

## Who must deduct TDS?

Most businesses, organisations and government bodies that make specified payments must deduct TDS when they pay. Individuals are often not required to deduct unless the payment relates to a business. [VERIFY]

| Payment type | Typical rate |
|---|---|

| House rent (paid to individuals) | [VERIFY: 10%] |
| Interest paid by banks to individuals | [VERIFY: 6%] |

| Salary | As per income tax slab rates |

> Rates change with each Finance Act. Always confirm the current rate before you deduct.

**Example:** You pay NPR 1,00,000 to a VAT-registered consultancy for services.
TDS at 1.5% = NPR 1,500 → pay the consultant NPR 98,500 (plus VAT on the invoice) and deposit NPR 1,500 with the IRD. [VERIFY: rate]

## Deadlines and how to file

1. Deduct TDS when you pay or accrue the payment.
2. Deposit it through the IRD's online system or the bank within [VERIFY: 25 days] of the month end.
3. File the **e-TDS return** with each payee's PAN so they receive credit.
4. Give payees TDS certificates where required.

## Common mistakes

- **Not collecting payees' PAN**, which creates higher rates and credit problems
- **Applying the wrong rate** (for example, treating a non-VAT supplier as VAT-registered)
- **Depositing tax without filing e-TDS**, so the payee never gets credit
- **Forgetting TDS on rent** paid for office premises
- **Missing payments to non-residents**, such as software subscriptions and foreign consultants

## Frequently asked questions

**What happens if I don't deduct TDS?**
The IRD can recover the undeducted tax from you, along with interest and fees. [VERIFY: provisions under Income Tax Act 2058]

**Is TDS a final tax?**
For some payments it is final withholding. For others, the payee claims it as a credit against their annual tax. [VERIFY: final withholding list]

**Do I deduct TDS on VAT?**
Generally TDS is calculated on the amount excluding VAT. [VERIFY]

**What if my supplier doesn't have a PAN?**
Consider asking them to obtain one. Different rules and limits apply to payments to suppliers without a PAN. [VERIFY]

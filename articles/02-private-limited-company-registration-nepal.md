# How to Register a Private Limited Company in Nepal

> **Quick answer:** Register a private limited company in Nepal online through the Office of the Company Registrar (OCR). Reserve a name, submit the Memorandum and Articles of Association with the promoters' documents, and pay the registration fee based on authorised capital. Then obtain a PAN from the IRD, open a bank account and complete local ward registration before you start trading.

Choosing a private limited company gives you limited liability, a separate legal identity and an easier path to bringing in investors. Here is the process and what comes after it.

## Step 1: Reserve your company name

- Apply on the OCR online portal (ocr.gov.np) with 2–3 name options.
- Names must not be identical or confusingly similar to existing companies and must end with "Private Limited".
- Approval is usually quick if the name is clearly unique. [VERIFY: validity period of name approval]

## Step 2: Prepare the documents

- Memorandum of Association (MoA): objectives, authorised, issued and paid-up capital
- Articles of Association (AoA): internal rules and director provisions
- Promoters' citizenship copies (passports and investment approvals for foreign promoters)
- Promoters' agreement, if there is more than one promoter
- Photos and signatures of promoters

**Tip:** Write the objectives clause carefully. Some activities need sector licences, and a vague objective can cause problems later with banks, licensing or FDI approval.

Upload the documents on the OCR portal. The fee depends on **authorised capital**: [VERIFY: current OCR fee slabs]

After verification, OCR issues the **Certificate of Incorporation**.

## Step 4: Get a PAN (and VAT if applicable)

Apply for a PAN through the IRD portal and complete verification at your tax office. If your expected turnover or sector requires VAT, register for VAT at the same time (see our [VAT registration guide](/vat-registration-in-nepal)).

## Step 5: Post-registration essentials

1. **Ward registration** at the local municipality where your office is located
2. **Bank account** in the company's name
3. **Share certificates** issued to shareholders
4. **Sector licences**, if your business needs them
5. **Social Security Fund (SSF) registration** if you employ staff [VERIFY: requirement]

## Annual compliance after registration

- Annual General Meeting and filing of annual returns with OCR [VERIFY: deadline]
- Audited financial statements and an income tax return each fiscal year
- Monthly VAT and TDS returns where applicable
- Updating OCR for changes in directors, shareholders, address or capital

Missing OCR annual filings can result in fines and, after prolonged default, action against the company. [VERIFY]

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does company registration take in Nepal?**
If documents are in order, registration can be completed within a few working days. [VERIFY]

**What is the minimum capital for a private limited company?**
There is no general minimum for most domestic private companies, but certain sectors and foreign investment have minimum thresholds. [VERIFY: current FDI minimum]

**Can a foreigner own a company in Nepal?**
Yes, subject to Foreign Investment and Technology Transfer Act approval, the minimum investment amount and the sectors open to foreign investment.
